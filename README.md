This project is an AI-powered resume tailoring tool designed to help users create job-specific resumes effortlessly. By integrating the **Gemini API**, the tool allows users to customize their resumes for each job application, ensuring they highlight the most relevant skills and experiences. Additionally, the tool provides an **AI review function** and calculates an **ATS score** to maximize the chances of passing Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS).

---

## Why I Built This

Most AI-powered resume tools are either **paid** or come with **limited functionality**, making it difficult for users to access high-quality resume tailoring and review features. I created this tool to provide a **free, open-source, and feature-rich alternative** that empowers job seekers to create tailored resumes, receive AI-driven feedback, and optimize their resumes for ATS systems—all without breaking the bank.

---

## Features

- **Gemini API Integration**: Leverage the power of Gemini AI to dynamically tailor resumes for specific job descriptions.
- **Job-Specific Customization**: Users can input a job description, and the tool will optimize their resume to match the requirements.
- **AI Review Function**: Get detailed feedback on your resume, including suggestions for improvement and areas of strength.
- **ATS Score**: Receive a score that indicates how well your resume is optimized for ATS systems.
- **User-Friendly Interface**: Simple and intuitive design for seamless resume building and editing.
- **Free and Open-Source**: No hidden costs or limitations—unlike many other AI resume tools.

---

## How It Works

1. **Input Your Detail**: Like experience ,education,skill.
2. **Input Job Description**: Paste the job description for the role you're applying to.
3. **AI Tailoring**: The Gemini API analyzes the job description and tailors your resume to match the keywords, skills, and requirements.
4. **AI Review**: The tool provides an AI-generated review of your resume, highlighting strengths and areas for improvement.
5. **ATS Score**: Get an ATS compatibility score to ensure your resume passes automated screening systems.
6. **Download Your Tailored Resume**: Export your optimized resume and start applying!
